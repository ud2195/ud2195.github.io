LayR — AI-powered digital closet & personal stylist

Founder•2025 → Present•Consumer AI

LayR turns your wardrobe into a personalized styling engine. Users upload their clothes once, and LayR builds a style profile (body shape + color palette + preferences) to generate outfits that fit real contexts — work, dates, travel, weddings — without the “I have nothing to wear” spiral.

  • Style Profile Engine: photo-based color analysis + body-shape inputs + preference capture → consistent personalization.
  • Wardrobe Intelligence: structured item understanding (metadata, seasons/occasions) + retrieval to surface the right pieces quickly.
  • Outfit Generation: occasion-aware outfit composition from owned items with saved looks + fast re-generation.
  • Retention Loops: upload → better outfits → save to plans/occasions → revisit → repeat; quality improves as the closet grows.
layr.style • App Store • YouTube (TikTok examples)

Turning AI demos into a real product

AI styling is easy to demo but hard to ship as a real product. The challenge is balancing quality, latency, and cost while keeping the experience fast enough that users actually come back.

  • Latency discipline: caching strategies, efficient orchestration, and selective generation so recommendations feel quick.
  • Quality iteration: prompt + pipeline improvements using real usage feedback to improve relevance over time.
  • Production infrastructure: AWS-first architecture (serverless + managed services) designed to scale cost-efficiently.
  • Growth-friendly outputs: content-ready results that translate well to short-form social formats.
